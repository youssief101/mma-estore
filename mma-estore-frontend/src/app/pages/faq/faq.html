Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

You will receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. You can also track your order in your account.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Cash on Delivery, and Gift Cards.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us within 24 hours of placing your order and we will do our best to accommodate your request.
